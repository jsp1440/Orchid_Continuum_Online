## Key Comprehensive Reviews Worth Incorporating

### 1. **Alkaloid Screening Programmes (1960s-1994)**
**Swedish Team (Bjorn Luning & Kurt Leander):**
- Extensive alkaloid screening of orchid species
- Published data on **1,750 orchid species by 1994**
- Overcame challenges of rare/small plant specimens
- Pioneering work in orchid phytochemistry

**Australian Team (Slaytor & Walker):**
- Parallel alkaloid research programme
- Complementary to Swedish work
- Combined: comprehensive alkaloid database

**Reference Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ESSENTIAL  
**Incorporation**: Add to phytochemical database, cite in compound research

---

### 2. **Chen, S.C. & Tang, T. (1982) - General Review of Orchid Flora of China**
- **Comprehensive taxonomic review** of Chinese orchids
- Foundation for understanding medicinal species distribution
- ~1,200 species in 174 genera documented in China
- **250+ species (~20%) have medicinal usage** in Chinese herbals

**Reference Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ESSENTIAL  
**Incorporation**: Critical for taxonomy validation and medicinal orchid identification

---

### 3. **Shen Nong Ben Cao Jing (Divine Farmer's Materia Medica)**
**Historical Significance:**
- **First century CE** - oldest Chinese medical text
- Described **365 medicinal substances** (237 botanical)
- **Three classical medicinal orchids**:
  1. **Tianma** (Gastrodia elata) - brain/neurological
  2. **Shihu** (Dendrobium spp.) - immune/longevity  
  3. **Baiji** (Bletilla striata) - wound healing

**50+ Revised Editions** over centuries (Hou, 1977)

**Reference Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ FOUNDATIONAL  
**Incorporation**: Essential historical context, classical TCM attribution

---

### 4. **Modern Chinese Materia Medica Expansions**
**Post-1949 Research:**
- "Bamboo Curtain" period forced China to develop indigenous medicine
- **Extensive ethno-medicine documentation** (previously only provincial/tribal)
- **Hundreds of orchid species added** to expanded Ben Cao
- Provincial knowledge systematically compiled

**Key Texts:**
- Zhongyao Da Cidian (Great Dictionary of Chinese Medicine)
- Zhonghua Bencao (Chinese Materia Medica)

**Reference Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ESSENTIAL  
**Incorporation**: Modern TCM applications, expanded species coverage

---

### 5. **Japanese & Korean Medicinal Orchid Research**
- Extensive publications on **Gastrodia elata** (Tian Ma/Cheonma)
- **Dendrobium species** pharmacology
- **Bletilla striata** biomedical applications
- Published in native languages (often missed by Western readers)

**Reference Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ HIGH  
**Incorporation**: Complement Chinese research, alternative perspectives

---

### 6. **Indian Ayurvedic & Tribal Medicine**
**Asthvargha Tonic**: Orchids = 50% of 8 items  
**Dendrobium plicatile** (Flickingeria fimbriata):
- **36+ Indian vernacular names** (32 in Sanskrit)
- Many names denote "life" - cultural significance
- **Sanjeevani candidate** - legendary Ramayana herb that restored life to dying hero
- **Truckloads imported** from neighboring countries

**Reference Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ CRITICAL (Conservation)  
**Incorporation**: Aphrodisiac trade documentation, conservation alerts

---

### 7. **Recent Breakthrough Discoveries**

#### **Calanthe arisanensis - Calanquinone A**
- **Potent anticancer activity** against:
  - Lung (A549), Prostate (PC-3, DU145), Colon (HCT-8)
  - Breast (MCF7), Nasopharyngeal (KB), Brain (glioblastoma)
- **Better drug resistance profile than paclitaxel** (cancer drug from Pacific yew)
- **Total synthesis achieved** (Lee et al., 2008)
- Induces s-phase arrest and apoptosis in cancer cells

**Reference Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ BREAKTHROUGH  
**Incorporation**: Feature prominently, potential therapeutic applications

#### **Cymbidium Agglutinin (CA) - Antiviral**
- **Strongly inhibits coronaviruses**: TGEV, IBV, FCoV, MHV
- **Arteriviruses**: EAV, PRRSV
- **Torovirus**: EBV
- Utrecht University research (van de Meer et al., 2007)

**Reference Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ BREAKTHROUGH (COVID-era relevance)  
**Incorporation**: Highlight antiviral research, modern applications

#### **Bletilla striata Starch - Medical Innovation**
- **Embolization therapy** for inoperable liver cancer
- **Drug delivery system development**
- Proven hemostatic and wound healing properties

**Reference Value**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ CLINICAL APPLICATION  
**Incorporation**: Modern medical technology, beyond traditional use

## Key Takeaways for Orchid Continuum

1. **No Single Consolidated Review Exists** - Teoh (2016) is the most comprehensive English source
2. **Multiple Language Sources** - Chinese, Japanese, Korean research often missed
3. **Historical + Modern** - Need both classical texts (Shen Nong) and modern research (Calanquinone A)
4. **Conservation Critical** - Medicinal trade threatens wild populations
5. **Clinical Validation Needed** - Most research is laboratory-based, not human trials

**Bottom Line**: Teoh (2016) + classical TCM texts + recent breakthroughs = comprehensive medicinal orchid knowledge base
