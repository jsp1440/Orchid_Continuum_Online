## Current Project: The Orchid Continuum

I am developing **The Orchid Continuum**, a research-grade digital platform for orchid taxonomy, conservation, and trait analysis. The platform currently integrates:

- **35,320 orchid species** from authoritative taxonomy databases
- **GBIF occurrence data** with geographic and temporal metadata
- **EOL image repository** (5.6M images from Zenodo record 17210269)
- **AI-powered image analysis** for morphological trait extraction
- **Statistical correlation discovery** for trait-environment relationships

## Purpose of This Request

While the **media_manifest_2020_02_04** dataset provides excellent image URLs and basic licensing metadata (6 fields: content_id, page_id, source_url, eol_url, license, copyright_owner), I am seeking access to the **rich phenotypic, morphological, and ecological trait data** that I have observed on individual EOL species pages but which was not included in the image manifest.

### Specific Data Fields of Interest

For orchid research and conservation applications, the following trait categories would be particularly valuable:

**Morphological Traits:**
- Flower morphology (labellum structure, column features, spur characteristics)
- Plant size and growth habit (epiphytic, terrestrial, lithophytic)
- Leaf characteristics (deciduous, evergreen, succulent)
- Root system traits (aerial roots, velamen structure)

**Ecological Traits:**
- Habitat preferences (elevation range, forest type, substrate)
- Geographic distribution patterns
- Pollinator relationships and reproductive biology
- Mycorrhizal associations (if available)

**Conservation & Phenology:**
- Flowering phenology (bloom season, duration)
- IUCN conservation status (if EOL maintains this)
- Threat factors and population trends
- Climate/microhabitat requirements

**Taxonomic & Descriptive:**
- Vernacular names in multiple languages
- Species descriptions and diagnostic features
- Historical range data
- Taxonomic synonyms and accepted names
