## CATEGORY 1: FLOWER MORPHOLOGY (35 terms)

### Anther
**Definition:** The pollen-bearing part of the stamen; in orchids, fused with the style to form the column.  
**Illustration:** Cross-section showing anther cap on column tip  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH - Essential for understanding orchid reproduction

### Anther cap (Operculum)
**Definition:** A protective covering over the pollinia; falls off when pollinia are removed.  
**Illustration:** Side view of column showing removable cap  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Callus
**Definition:** A raised ridge or wartlike projection on the lip, often near the column.  
**Illustration:** Close-up of labellum showing callus structures  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH - Common feature students need to identify

### Column
**Definition:** The fused structure of stamens and pistil unique to orchids; central reproductive organ.  
**Illustration:** Front, side, and cross-section views with labeled parts  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** CRITICAL - Most distinctive orchid feature

### Column foot
**Definition:** A basal extension of the column to which the lip is attached.  
**Illustration:** Side profile showing column foot attachment  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Dorsal sepal
**Definition:** The uppermost sepal in an orchid flower, usually the largest.  
**Illustration:** Labeled flower diagram highlighting dorsal sepal  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Labellum (Lip)
**Definition:** The modified petal that serves as a landing platform for pollinators; usually the largest, most colorful petal.  
**Illustration:** Multiple lip types showing diversity (fringed, pouched, lobed)  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** CRITICAL - Key identification feature

### Lateral sepals
**Definition:** The two sepals flanking the lip in most orchid flowers.  
**Illustration:** Flower diagram showing lateral sepal positions  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Mentum (Chin)
**Definition:** A chinlike projection formed by fusion of column foot and lateral sepals.  
**Illustration:** Side view showing mentum structure  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Nectar spur
**Definition:** A tubular extension of the lip that produces or stores nectar.  
**Illustration:** Cross-section showing spur and nectar location  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Petal
**Definition:** The inner whorl of flower parts; in orchids, usually two lateral petals plus the modified lip.  
**Illustration:** Flower diagram labeling all three petals  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Pollinia (singular: pollinium)
**Definition:** Waxy pollen masses unique to orchids; attached to pollinators as a unit.  
**Illustration:** Close-up of pollinia with caudicle and viscidium  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** CRITICAL - Unique orchid characteristic

### Resupinate
**Definition:** Twisted 180° so the lip is on the bottom; most orchids are resupinate.  
**Illustration:** Before/after diagram showing flower twist during development  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH - Important identification concept

### Rostellum
**Definition:** A projection from the column between anther and stigma; prevents self-pollination.  
**Illustration:** Column cross-section highlighting rostellum position  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Sepal
**Definition:** Outer whorl of flower parts; orchids have three sepals.  
**Illustration:** Labeled diagram showing all three sepals  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Spur
**Definition:** See Nectar spur  
**Category:** morphology_flower

### Staminode
**Definition:** A sterile stamen; in slipper orchids (*Paphiopedilum*), the large shield-shaped structure.  
**Illustration:** Paphiopedilum flower showing prominent staminode  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH - Distinctive slipper orchid feature

### Stigma
**Definition:** The receptive surface for pollen; in orchids, a sticky cavity on the column.  
**Illustration:** Column section showing stigmatic surface  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Synsepal
**Definition:** Lateral sepals fused together; common in slipper orchids.  
**Illustration:** Paphiopedilum showing fused synsepal  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Tepals
**Definition:** Collective term for sepals and petals when they look similar.  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Viscidium (plural: viscidia)
**Definition:** The sticky disc that attaches pollinia to pollinators.  
**Illustration:** Close-up of pollinia apparatus showing viscidium  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Caudicle
**Definition:** The stalk connecting pollinia to the viscidium.  
**Illustration:** Pollinia structure with caudicle labeled  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Pouch
**Definition:** The slipper-shaped lip of *Paphiopedilum*, *Phragmipedium*, and *Cypripedium*.  
**Illustration:** Cross-section of slipper showing pouch structure  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Keiki
**Definition:** Hawaiian word for "baby"; a plantlet growing on parent plant (common in *Dendrobium*, *Phalaenopsis*).  
**Illustration:** Photo showing keiki with roots on flower spike  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH - Important propagation concept

### Inflorescence
**Definition:** The complete flower cluster with its stem and branches.  
**Illustration:** Different types: spike, raceme, panicle  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Bract
**Definition:** A modified leaf at the base of a flower or inflorescence.  
**Illustration:** Flower spike showing bracts at flower bases  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Raceme
**Definition:** An unbranched inflorescence with flowers on short stalks along main stem.  
**Illustration:** *Phalaenopsis* flower spike diagram  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Panicle
**Definition:** A branched inflorescence; flowers on secondary branches.  
**Illustration:** *Oncidium* branching flower spike  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Spike
**Definition:** An unbranched inflorescence with stalkless flowers.  
**Illustration:** Simple spike diagram  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Pedicel
**Definition:** The stalk of an individual flower.  
**Illustration:** Labeled showing pedicel vs peduncle  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Peduncle
**Definition:** The main stalk of an inflorescence.  
**Illustration:** Labeled inflorescence showing peduncle  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Scape
**Definition:** A leafless flower stalk arising from ground level.  
**Illustration:** Terrestrial orchid showing scape  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Ovary
**Definition:** The seed-bearing structure; in orchids, typically inferior (below flower parts).  
**Illustration:** Cross-section showing ovary position  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Capsule
**Definition:** The dry seed pod of orchids; splits along 3 or 6 lines when ripe.  
**Illustration:** Developing capsule and mature split pod  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Seed
**Definition:** Dustlike orchid seeds lack endosperm; require mycorrhizal fungi for germination in nature.  
**Illustration:** Microscopic view showing tiny seeds  
**Category:** morphology_flower  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

## CATEGORY 2: VEGETATIVE MORPHOLOGY (30 terms)

### Aerial roots
**Definition:** Roots growing above ground; absorb moisture and nutrients from air.  
**Illustration:** Epiphytic orchid showing green aerial roots  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH - Very visible feature

### Cane
**Definition:** An elongated pseudobulb (e.g., *Dendrobium*).  
**Illustration:** Dendrobium cane with leaves  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Conduplicate
**Definition:** Leaf folded lengthwise along midrib (V-shaped in cross-section).  
**Illustration:** Cross-section showing folded leaf  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Coriaceous
**Definition:** Thick and leathery leaf texture.  
**Illustration:** Comparison of coriaceous vs thin leaves  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Deciduous
**Definition:** Leaves or pseudobulbs that fall off seasonally.  
**Illustration:** Seasonal cycle showing leaf drop  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Equitant
**Definition:** Leaves folded at base, overlapping in two ranks (fan-shaped).  
**Illustration:** *Oncidium* equitant growth habit  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Leaf
**Definition:** The primary photosynthetic organ; highly variable in orchids.  
**Illustration:** Diversity of leaf types (terete, plicate, succulent)  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Monopodial
**Definition:** Growth from a single growing point at apex; grows taller each year.  
**Illustration:** Vertical growth diagram (*Phalaenopsis*, *Vanda*)  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** CRITICAL - Key growth pattern

### Node
**Definition:** The point on a stem where leaves or branches emerge.  
**Illustration:** Stem section showing nodes and internodes  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Plicate
**Definition:** Leaves with parallel pleats or folds (accordion-like).  
**Illustration:** Close-up of pleated leaf surface  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Pseudobulb
**Definition:** Thickened above-ground stem for water/nutrient storage.  
**Illustration:** Various shapes (round, cane, conical)  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** CRITICAL - Diagnostic feature

### Rhizome
**Definition:** Horizontal stem connecting growths in sympodial orchids.  
**Illustration:** Sympodial plant showing rhizome connections  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Root
**Definition:** Absorptive and anchoring structure; in epiphytes, covered with velamen.  
**Illustration:** Root cross-section showing velamen layers  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Sheath
**Definition:** Tubular structure surrounding and protecting new growth.  
**Illustration:** Cattleya sheath enclosing developing bud  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Sympodial
**Definition:** Growth from a series of adjacent growths; rhizomatous.  
**Illustration:** Horizontal growth diagram (*Cattleya*, *Oncidium*)  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** CRITICAL - Key growth pattern

### Terete
**Definition:** Cylindrical or pencil-shaped leaves (circular in cross-section).  
**Illustration:** *Vanda* terete leaf cross-section  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Tuber
**Definition:** Underground storage organ; common in terrestrial orchids.  
**Illustration:** Terrestrial orchid with tubers  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Unifoliate
**Definition:** Producing one leaf per pseudobulb.  
**Illustration:** Cattleya unifoliate growth  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Bifoliate
**Definition:** Producing two leaves per pseudobulb.  
**Illustration:** Cattleya bifoliate growth  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Velamen
**Definition:** Spongy outer layer of epiphytic orchid roots; absorbs water and nutrients.  
**Illustration:** Microscopic view of velamen cells  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH - Unique orchid adaptation

### Backbulb
**Definition:** Old pseudobulb behind current growth; often leafless but viable.  
**Illustration:** Series showing new to old pseudobulbs  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM - Important for propagation

### Growth
**Definition:** A single stem with its leaves and roots; the basic unit in sympodial orchids.  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Lead
**Definition:** The newest, actively growing pseudobulb.  
**Illustration:** Diagram showing lead vs backbulbs  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Eye
**Definition:** A dormant bud that can develop into new growth.  
**Illustration:** Close-up showing dormant eyes on pseudobulb  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Crown
**Definition:** The growing point at the stem apex in monopodial orchids.  
**Illustration:** Phalaenopsis crown with new leaf emerging  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Offset
**Definition:** A new plant developing at the base of parent plant.  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Stolon
**Definition:** A horizontal stem producing new plants at nodes or tips.  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Succulent
**Definition:** Thick, fleshy leaves or stems adapted for water storage.  
**Illustration:** Comparison showing succulent orchid structures  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Corm
**Definition:** A swollen underground stem base used for storage.  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Internode
**Definition:** The stem section between two nodes.  
**Category:** morphology_vegetative  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

## CATEGORY 3: ECOLOGY & HABITAT (28 terms)

### Epiphyte
**Definition:** A plant growing on another plant for support (not parasitic).  
**Illustration:** Orchid on tree branch showing aerial roots  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** CRITICAL - Most orchids are epiphytic

### Lithophyte
**Definition:** A plant growing on rocks or cliffs.  
**Illustration:** Orchid growing on rock outcrop  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Terrestrial
**Definition:** Growing in soil or ground-level organic matter.  
**Illustration:** *Paphiopedilum* in forest floor litter  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Saprophyte
**Definition:** A plant lacking chlorophyll, obtaining nutrients from decaying organic matter.  
**Illustration:** Ghostly white saprophytic orchid  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Mycorrhiza (plural: mycorrhizae)
**Definition:** Symbiotic fungi living in/on orchid roots; essential for seed germination.  
**Illustration:** Root section showing fungal threads  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH - Critical biological relationship

### Protocorm
**Definition:** The early developmental stage of orchid seedling before first leaf.  
**Illustration:** Germination sequence from seed to protocorm  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Cloud forest
**Definition:** High-elevation tropical forest with persistent fog; rich in orchid diversity.  
**Illustration:** Misty forest habitat with epiphytes  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Canopy
**Definition:** The upper tree layer where many epiphytic orchids grow.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Endemic
**Definition:** Native to a specific geographic region and found nowhere else.  
**Illustration:** Map showing endemic species range  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Pollinator
**Definition:** An animal (insect, bird, bat) that transfers pollen between flowers.  
**Illustration:** Various pollinators on orchid flowers  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Pollination syndrome
**Definition:** Flower characteristics adapted to specific pollinator types.  
**Illustration:** Chart matching flower traits to pollinators  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Deceptive pollination
**Definition:** Attracting pollinators without providing rewards (no nectar).  
**Illustration:** Examples of mimicry and deception  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Fragrance
**Definition:** Scent produced to attract pollinators; varies by time of day.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Photoperiod
**Definition:** Day length; influences flowering in some orchids.  
**Illustration:** Graph showing seasonal light changes  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Monsoon
**Definition:** Seasonal heavy rainfall period in tropical/subtropical regions.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Dry season
**Definition:** Period of reduced rainfall triggering flowering in many orchids.  
**Illustration:** Seasonal cycle diagram  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Altitudinal range
**Definition:** The elevation range where a species naturally occurs.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Microhabitat
**Definition:** The specific small-scale environment where an organism lives.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Native
**Definition:** Naturally occurring in a region without human introduction.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Naturalized
**Definition:** Non-native species established and reproducing in the wild.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Invasive
**Definition:** Non-native species spreading aggressively and harming ecosystems.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Keystone species
**Definition:** A species with disproportionate effect on its ecosystem.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Biodiversity hotspot
**Definition:** Region with exceptional species concentration and high threat levels.  
**Illustration:** World map showing hotspots  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Habitat fragmentation
**Definition:** Breaking continuous habitat into smaller, isolated patches.  
**Illustration:** Diagram showing forest fragmentation  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Symbiosis
**Definition:** Close, long-term interaction between different species.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Commensalism
**Definition:** Relationship benefiting one species without affecting the other.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Mutualism
**Definition:** Relationship benefiting both species involved.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Competition
**Definition:** Interaction where organisms vie for limited resources.  
**Category:** ecology  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

## CATEGORY 4: CULTIVATION & HORTICULTURE (40 terms)

### Medium (Media)
**Definition:** The material orchids are potted in (bark, sphagnum, etc.).  
**Illustration:** Photos of different media types  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH - Essential knowledge

### Bark
**Definition:** Chunky tree bark (usually fir or pine) used as potting medium.  
**Illustration:** Different bark grades (fine, medium, coarse)  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Sphagnum moss
**Definition:** Water-retentive moss used as potting medium or moisture retention layer.  
**Illustration:** Fresh vs dried sphagnum  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Charcoal
**Definition:** Horticultural charcoal added to media for drainage and to prevent souring.  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Perlite
**Definition:** Volcanic glass particles added for aeration and drainage.  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Humidity
**Definition:** Atmospheric moisture level; most orchids prefer 50-80%.  
**Illustration:** Hygrometer showing ideal ranges  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Light
**Definition:** Illumination intensity; requirements vary by orchid type.  
**Illustration:** Chart showing light levels for different genera  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Temperature
**Definition:** Heat levels; orchids categorized as warm, intermediate, or cool growing.  
**Illustration:** Temperature range chart by type  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Fertilizer
**Definition:** Nutrients added to promote growth; orchids need dilute, balanced formulas.  
**Illustration:** NPK ratio guide  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Watering
**Definition:** Providing moisture; most orchids prefer "dry between waterings."  
**Illustration:** Visual guide to when to water  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Air movement

**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Repotting

**Illustration:** Step-by-step repotting guide

**Visual Priority:** HIGH

### Root pruning

### Division

### Mounting

**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Greenhouse
**Definition:** Controlled environment structure for growing orchids.  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Shade cloth
**Definition:** Fabric reducing light intensity to prevent leaf burn.  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Rest period
**Definition:** Seasonal reduction in water/fertilizer to trigger flowering.  
**Illustration:** Annual growth cycle showing rest period  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** MEDIUM

### Acclimation
**Definition:** Gradual adjustment to new growing conditions.  
**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Hardening off

**Category:** cultivation  
**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Flush

**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Stake

**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Clip

**Visual Priority:** LOW

### Meristem culture

### Specimen plant

### Award
