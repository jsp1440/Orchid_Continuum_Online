- **Type:** Conceptual illustration with examples
- **Specs:** 800×600px, show diversity (trees, flowers, grasses, orchids)
- **Use Case:** Helps seniors understand broadest classification level

## 032 - Division (Visual Definition)
- **Term:** Division - Major group (Magnoliophyta = flowering plants)
- **Visual:** Split diagram: flowering vs non-flowering plants
- **Type:** Comparison illustration
- **Specs:** Show orchid in flowering category
- **Use Case:** Clarifies flowering plant category

## 033 - Class (Visual Definition)
- **Term:** Class - Subdivision (Liliopsida = monocots)
- **Visual:** Monocot vs dicot comparison with orchid highlighted
- **Type:** Side-by-side botanical comparison
- **Specs:** Show parallel veins, single cotyledon, flower parts in 3s
- **Use Case:** Teach monocot identification

## 034 - Order (Visual Definition)
- **Term:** Order - Grouping of families (Asparagales includes orchids)
- **Visual:** Show Asparagales families with orchids highlighted
- **Type:** Family tree diagram
- **Specs:** Include Asparagus, Iris, Orchid family relationships
- **Use Case:** Show orchid relatives

## 035 - Family (Visual Definition)
- **Term:** Family - Group of related genera (Orchidaceae)
- **Visual:** Orchidaceae characteristics checklist with photos
- **Type:** Annotated photo collage
- **Specs:** Show column, pollinia, resupinate flowers, bilateral symmetry
- **Use Case:** Teach "what makes an orchid an orchid"

## 036 - Subfamily/Tribe/Subtribe (Visual Definition)

- **Use Case:** Show species consistency despite minor variation
