## 🎨 DESIGN STANDARDS (ALL ILLUSTRATIONS)

### **Glossary Cards (800×600px):**
- **Style:** Clean, educational, icon/diagram-based
- **Colors:** FCOS green (#42a844), Orchid purple (#a020f0)
- **Fonts:** Montserrat Bold (titles 24-28pt), Open Sans (body ≥18pt)
- **Layout:** Term as title, visual in center, brief definition below
- **Accessibility:** High contrast, large text, senior-friendly
- **Multilingual Ready:** Minimal text, universal visuals

### **Educational Diagrams (1200×900px or specified):**
- **Complete specs in source files** - each has step-by-step Canva instructions
- **Scientific accuracy required** - verify against Kew POWO, WCVP, APG IV
- **Senior-friendly** - large labels, clear hierarchies, no excessive detail

### **Botanical Drawings (1000×800px):**
- **Scientific illustration style** - line drawings with labels
- **Use database images as reference** - 10,534 GBIF photos available
- **Show key identification features** - morphology, growth habit, distinctive traits

### **Tutorial Graphics (1200×900px):**
- **Step-by-step format** - numbered sequences
- **Practice examples included** - good vs poor technique comparisons
- **Printable quality** - suitable for workshop handouts
