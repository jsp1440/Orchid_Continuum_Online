## Getting Started

### What is Orchid Continuum?

The Orchid Continuum is an academic botanical research platform that integrates:
- **35,320 orchid taxonomy entries** from authoritative sources
- **5.8+ million images** from Encyclopedia of Life
- **104,000+ wild occurrence images** from GBIF
- **4.2+ million herbarium specimens** from Tropicos/Missouri Botanical Garden
- **AI-powered analysis** for trait discovery and identification
- **Interactive widgets** for education and engagement

### Who is it for?

- 🔬 **Researchers**: Academic-grade data for botanical research
- 📚 **Students**: Educational resources for mycorrhizal networks, AI-biology, conservation
- 🌸 **Enthusiasts**: Orchid identification, care guides, community features
- 👨‍🏫 **Educators**: Interactive tools for teaching botany and ecology

## Core Features

### 1. Orchid Search & Browse

**Find any orchid in our database:**

- **Search by name**: Scientific or common names
- **Filter by genus**: Browse all species in a genus
- **Geographic filter**: Find orchids by country or region
- **Advanced search**: Combine multiple filters

**How to use:**
1. Go to main search page
2. Enter orchid name or use filters
3. Click on results to see detailed information
4. View images, habitat data, and research notes

### 2. Image Galleries

**Explore millions of orchid images:**

- **Main Gallery**: Browse all orchid images
- **Featured Galleries**: Curated collections
- **Geographic Galleries**: Images by location (Thailand, Madagascar, etc.)
- **Themed Galleries**: Fragrant orchids, night-blooming orchids, etc.

**Gallery Hub** (`/gallery-hub`):
- Thailand Orchids
- Madagascar Orchids  
- Fragrant Orchids
- Night-Blooming Orchids

### 3. AI-Powered Identification

**Upload a photo, get instant ID:**

1. Go to AI Identification tool
2. Upload your orchid photo
3. AI analyzes flower structure, color, patterns
4. Get suggested species with confidence scores
5. View similar species for comparison

**What the AI looks for:**
- Flower shape and symmetry
- Color patterns
- Spur length and shape
- Petal/sepal arrangement
- Lip characteristics

### 4. Research Data Access

**Access comprehensive orchid data:**

- **Taxonomy**: Scientific names, authors, synonyms
- **Distribution**: Where species are found globally
- **Habitat**: Elevation, temperature, rainfall data
- **Traits**: Morphological characteristics from TraitBank
- **Images**: Wild specimens with full metadata

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem: Images not loading**
- **Solution**: Refresh page, check internet connection
- **Reason**: Large image files may load slowly

**Problem: AI identification incorrect**
- **Solution**: Try different photo angle, ensure flower is in focus
- **Note**: AI provides suggestions, not definitive IDs

**Problem: Search returns no results**
- **Solution**: Check spelling, try genus name only, use wildcards
- **Example**: Search "Phal*" for Phalaenopsis species

**Problem: Widget not working**
- **Solution**: Clear browser cache, try different browser
- **Supported**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions)

### Getting Help

**Bug Reports:**
1. Go to `/bug-report`
2. Describe the issue
3. Include steps to reproduce
4. Optional: Provide email for follow-up

**Documentation:**
- Full guides in `/docs` directory
