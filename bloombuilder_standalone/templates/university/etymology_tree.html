Understanding Etymology

Etymology is the study of word origins and how their meanings have evolved over time.

Most botanical terms combine Greek or Latin roots to describe plant characteristics:

  • Prefix (beginning): Modifies the meaning → epi- (upon)
  • Root (middle): Core meaning → phyte (plant)
  • Suffix (ending): Grammatical function → -ic (relating to)

Example: epi-phyt-ic = "relating to plants growing upon (other plants)"
