What We're Building

The Orchid Continuum integrates authoritative taxonomy databases (GBIF, Tropicos, EOL), AI-powered image analysis, and ecological pattern discovery into a unified research platform. We're preserving orchid biodiversity data that could be at risk while making it accessible for scientific research, education, and practical cultivation.

Why Complete Species Coverage Matters

  • Conservation: With 56% of orchids threatened with extinction, comprehensive documentation is critical for preservation efforts
  • Research: AI vision analysis requires diverse training data across species, genera, and families to identify taxonomic patterns
  • Climate Analysis: Geographic distribution data helps predict habitat changes and cultivation zones
  • Cultivation Science: Linking wild habitat data to growing conditions helps growers replicate natural environments
  • Educational Value: Complete species coverage provides students and researchers with comparative morphology examples

Baker's Cultural Guidelines
Light

2500-4000 foot-candles. Provide bright light similar to Cattleya requirements. Leaves should be light green, not dark.

Temperature

Warm to intermediate. Summer days 28-32°C, nights 20-24°C. Winter rest with cooler temps (18-22°C days, 15-18°C nights).

Water

Heavy watering during active growth (spring-fall). Allow to dry between waterings during winter rest period after flowering.

Humidity

60-70% year-round with good air circulation. Lower humidity acceptable during winter rest.

Fertilizer

Weekly during active growth at 1/4 strength balanced formula (20-20-20). Reduce to monthly during rest.

Potting

Coarse bark mix or mounted. Repot every 2-3 years after flowering, as new growth emerges.

Growing Tip: The key to flowering is providing a distinct dry rest period after canes mature in fall. Reduce watering dramatically and provide cooler nights to trigger blooming.

AI Vision Analysis: Where Your Collection Makes the Biggest Impact

The Digital Botanist Vision AI system is one of our most critical research tools. Here's how it works and why your collection is essential to its success.

What the AI Vision System Does

Our AI analyzes orchid photographs to:

  • Identify species from morphological features (flower shape, lip structure, column characteristics)
  • Extract botanical terminology - describing plants using proper scientific vocabulary (1,763 botanical terms)
  • Generate scientific illustrations - creating line drawings, labeled diagrams, and watercolor renderings
  • Correlate habitat data - linking visual characteristics to growing conditions
  • Assist conservation - identifying rare/threatened species from field photos
2-Phase Training Process

Phase 1: Learning Botanical Language

  • 1,648 Tropicos herbarium specimens analyzed
  • 1,763 botanical terms learned and applied
  • Supervised learning from expert-verified specimens

Phase 2: Wild Identification Testing

  • 7,373 iNaturalist wild orchid photos tested
  • Blind identification without prior training
  • Real-world conditions (varying light, angles, quality)
