How BloomBuilder Works

Select a species → authentic herbarium sheets anchor your study.
Observe & compare → inspect high-resolution images against historical plates.
Identify & label → apply dichotomous keys and orchid terminology.
Verify through traits → consult the Trait Databank for diagnostic matches.
Add notes → record insights, hypotheses, and pollinator-linked observations.
Explore adaptation → relate structures to pollination, habitat, and survival.
Create & contribute → generate a final composite and add it to the Continuum.
